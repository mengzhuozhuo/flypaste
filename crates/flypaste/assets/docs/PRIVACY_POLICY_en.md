# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: June 19, 2026

Welcome to Flypaste. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Flypaste macOS application.

## 1. Data Collection & Storage

**We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data or clipboard history.**

Flypaste is a fully local application. All your clipboard data (text, images, files) is stored locally on your Mac and is strictly confined to Apple's highly secure **App Sandbox** environment. The data never leaves your device, and we have no servers that receive your data.

## 2. Accessibility Permissions

Flypaste requires macOS Accessibility permissions (System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Accessibility) to function properly.

**Why we need this permission:**
We use this permission **solely** to simulate the "Command + V" (Paste) keystroke. When you select an item from your Flypaste history, the app automatically pastes it into your currently active application. We do not use this permission to monitor your keystrokes (keylogging) or to read data from other applications.

## 3. Network Usage

Flypaste does not require an internet connection to perform its core clipboard management functions.
